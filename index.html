1. Area Information: Zoom in the map to your desired location, then click to declare the region that you want to consider. You can drag the bottom-right corner of the map to change the region ratio. Make sure the region is 25km² or smaller.

2. Soil Information: Click to calculate current soil properties in the area selected above.

3. Climate Information: Click to calculate climate estimates with historical climates from to 2025.

4. Crop Information: Select crops to consider for rotation below, then click to calculate the most suitable crop. Growing season ranges: week to week and (optionally, for southern-hemisphere regions) week to week
